Features

A focused toolkit for planning meals and tracking progress.

Food list

Save foods with calories, macros, serving weight, and notes so repeat meals are easier to plan.

Calorie target

Estimate daily calorie needs from profile details and activity level, then keep the target visible while planning.

BMI reference

Check body mass index and ideal-weight ranges as a simple reference, with profile values shared across the app.

Recipe library

Browse structured meal ideas with ingredients, steps, nutrition context, and dietary tags.

Meal prep

Build meal-prep plans by calorie level and turn selected recipes into a more organized week.

Shopping lists

Collect ingredients into list-ready groups so grocery planning is easier to scan and act on.

Privacy Policy

Mealful privacy policy

Overview

Mealful is a personal meal planning and nutrition reference app. The app is designed to store your planning data on your device.

Data you enter

You may enter food items, meal plans, recipe preferences, BMI and calorie calculator values, profile values, shopping lists, theme choices, language choices, and weight entries.

Data collection

Mealful does not require an account and does not currently send your personal app data to a developer-operated server.

Third parties

Mealful does not currently include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Purchases

Mealful Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process purchase information according to Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

Device storage

Your app data may remain on your device unless you delete the app, clear app data, or use operating-system backup and restore features controlled by Apple.

Health and nutrition note

Mealful provides planning tools and general calculation references. It is not medical advice and should not replace guidance from a qualified health professional.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the App Store support listing for Mealful.
